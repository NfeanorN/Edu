Exercise 2 (8 pts) — Division A & B

Allocate shared costs, then: Pre-tax profit = Revenue − all costs · Tax = 30% · Net Income = Pre-tax − Tax

Formula for each shared cost:
Division share = Total cost × (Division base ÷ Total base)

Then:
Pre-tax profit = Revenue − Direct labour − Indirect labour − Raw materials − Selling − Electricity − Financial − Advertising
Tax = Pre-tax × 30%
Net Income = Pre-tax − Tax

Step 1 — allocate shared costs (empty cells in exam table):

  1. Indirect labour (total 8,400) ÷ by Direct labour (16 + 32 = 48)
    A = 8,400 × 16/48 = 2,800 · B = 8,400 × 32/48 = 5,600
  2. Selling (total 24,000) ÷ by Revenue (144 + 120 = 264)
    A = 24,000 × 144/264 = 13,091 · B = 24,000 × 120/264 = 10,909
  3. Advertising (total 25,000) ÷ by Revenue
    A = 25,000 × 144/264 = 13,636 · B = 25,000 × 120/264 = 11,364
  4. Electricity (total 22,400) ÷ by Raw materials (28 + 32 = 60)
    A = 22,400 × 28/60 = 10,453 · B = 22,400 × 32/60 = 11,947
  5. Financial (total 30,000) ÷ by Raw materials
    A = 30,000 × 28/60 = 14,000 · B = 30,000 × 32/60 = 16,000

Exercise 3 (8 pts) — DCF Cassino SpA

Given: WACC = 5% · g = 1% · Cash flows 2024–2028 as on exam sheet

Discount coefficient (year n) = 1 ÷ (1 + WACC)n
Discounted cash flow = Cash flow × Discount coefficient
W = sum of all discounted cash flows
  1. 2024 (year 1): 1/1.05 = 0.9524 → 25,000 × 0.9524 = 23,810
  2. 2025 (year 2): 1/1.05² = 0.9070 → 30,000 × 0.9070 = 27,211
  3. 2026 (year 3): 1/1.05³ = 0.8638 → 35,000 × 0.8638 = 30,234
  4. 2027 (year 4): 1/1.05⁴ = 0.8227 → 38,000 × 0.8227 = 31,263
  5. 2028 TV (year 5): 1/1.05⁵ = 0.7835 → 40,000 × 0.7835 = 31,341
