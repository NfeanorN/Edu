(b) For each method, build a 6-year schedule: cost, accumulated depreciation, NBV.

(a) Annual depreciation charge — answer

Straight-line

Formula: (Cost − Residual) ÷ Life = (160,000 − 28,000) ÷ 6

Annual depreciation = $22,000 every year (same in years 1–6)
