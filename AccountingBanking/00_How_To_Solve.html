MCQ · Test 03

1. ROE (Return on Equity)

Given: bank capital = 50, profit = 5

Дано: капитал банка = 50, прибыль = 5

ROE = Net profit ÷ Bank capital
  1. Check if profit is already net (after tax) or pre-tax.
  2. Divide: ROE = 5 ÷ 50 = 0.10 = 10%
Answer: b) 10%
Trap: If profit is pre-tax and tax = 50% → net profit = 2.5 → ROE = 5% (answer a).
MCQ · Test 03

2. Repricing gap — change in NII

Given: Gap = −10,000,000 €, interest rate change = +2%

ΔNII = Gap × Δr
  1. Convert rate: 2% = 0.02
  2. ΔNII = (−10,000,000) × 0.02 = −200,000 €
  3. Negative gap + rising rates → NII falls
Answer: a) −200,000 €
MCQ · Test 03

3. Which bond has the longest duration?

Duration is higher when:

  • Maturity is longer (10 years > 5 years > 1 year)
  • Coupons are paid less often (annual > monthly)

Compare: 10 years + annual coupon beats 10 years + monthly coupon.

Answer: c) 10 years, 5%, coupon paid annually

5. Division table — allocate shared costs

Rule: split each common cost using the base shown in the question.

Cost lineAllocate byExample (2024)
Indirect labourDirect labourA:B = 12:24
Selling, AdvertisingRevenueA:B = 108:72
Electricity, FinancialRaw materialsA:B = 12:6

Formula for one division:

Share = Total cost × (Division base ÷ Sum of bases)

Then:

Pre-tax profit = Revenue − all costs (direct + allocated) Tax = 30% × Pre-tax Net Income = Pre-tax − Tax

Example — Division A (2024):

  1. Selling = 12,000 × 108/180 = 7,200
  2. Electricity = 16,800 × 12/18 = 11,200
  3. Pre-tax = 48,400 → Tax = 14,520 → Net = 33,880
Division A: Net Income 33,880 · Division B: Net Income 14,840

6. Production budget (30% ending inventory)

Given: Sales May = 90,000 · Sales June = 80,000 · ending inventory = 30% of next month's sales

Ending inv. = 30% × next month sales Beginning inv. = 30% × current month sales Production = Sales + Ending inv. − Beginning inv.
  1. Ending (May) = 0.30 × 80,000 = 24,000
  2. Beginning (May) = 0.30 × 90,000 = 27,000
  3. Production = 90,000 + 24,000 − 27,000 = 87,000 units
Production May = 87,000 units

11. Theory — short answers (for oral/written exam)

Functions of the financial system
Mobilise savings; channel funds to borrowers; allocate risk; transform maturities and volumes; payments; price information.
Why financial intermediaries exist
Lower transaction costs; diversification; information asymmetry (adverse selection, moral hazard); maturity transformation; expertise.
Bank balance sheet & key ratios
Assets: loans, securities, reserves. Liabilities: deposits, borrowings. Capital. Ratios: ROE, ROA, NIM, CAR, LCR, NPL.
Interest rate risk
Rate changes affect NII and asset/liability values. Manage with repricing gap, duration, hedging.
DCF model
Forecast FCF → discount (WACC or Re via CAPM) → terminal value → firm/equity value.
