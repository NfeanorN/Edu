S1-Q1 · Elevator / Deck / Video / Summary / Plan / BMC

Elevator pitch — 30–60 sec spoken idea. Pitch deck — slides for investors. Video pitch — short video. Executive summary — 1–2 page text. Business plan — full detailed plan. BMC — one page: how the business makes money.

Use elevator/video for fast intro; deck/summary for meetings; plan for deep detail; BMC to show the model clearly.

2
S1-Q2 · TRL, BRL, IRL

TRL = Technology Readiness Level — how ready the technology is (idea → real product).

BRL = Business Readiness Level — how ready the business is (market, sales, team).

IRL = Investment Readiness Level — how ready the startup is for investment.

Purpose: see risks and readiness before money/market steps.

3
S1-Q3 · Key sections of a pitch deck

Main parts: problem, solution, market, product, business model, competition, team, finance, ask (how much money).

Investors need a fast clear picture: why it matters, who pays, how you make money, and who builds it.

4
S1-Q4 · Valuation formulas

Post-money = Investment ÷ Ownership %

Pre-money = Post-money − Investment

Ownership % = Investment ÷ Post-money

Exit proceeds = Exit value × Ownership %

These show who owns what and who gets what when the company is sold. Key for founders and investors.

5
S1-B1 · Exit €50m · 60% / 40%

Formula: Exit proceeds = Exit value × Ownership %

Founder 60% → €30,000,000

Investor 40% → €20,000,000

6
S1-B2 · €2.5m for 25%

Post-money = 2.5 ÷ 0.25 = €10m (value after investment)

Pre-money = 10 − 2.5 = €7.5m (value before investment)

S2-Q1 · Tech transfer & carbon

Digital tech that helps: AI, IoT, big data (and smart platforms) — lower carbon intensity, better efficiency.

Tech transfer shares know-how, tools and training so countries/firms can use clean solutions faster and cut emissions.

8
S2-Q2 · FinTech & sustainability

Tech transfer helps FinTech with green finance tools, monitoring and digital reporting — easier to follow environmental rules.

FinTech supports growth: cheaper payments/credit, more access to money, and funding for sustainable projects.

9
S3-Q1 · Business ecosystem roots & features

A business ecosystem is a network of firms, startups, universities and investors around shared value.

Roots: not only buyer–seller deals, but long ties and co-creating value.

Core features: mutual dependence, shared resources, joint innovation, win-win logic.

10
S3-Q2 · Five arguments · ecosystems & innovation

1) Share knowledge and tech.

2) Test ideas faster on the market.

3) Easier to find partners and customers.

4) Startups get money and infrastructure.

5) Risks and costs of innovation are shared.

11
S4-Q1 · Mind Map · tools

Mind Map = idea map around one central topic. Good for fast idea generation and seeing links. Limit: can get messy, weak for deep analysis.

Apps (max 5): Miro, XMind, MindMeister, Coggle, Microsoft Whiteboard.

12
S4-Q2 · NABC model

NABC = Need, Approach, Benefits, Competition.

Purpose: explain an innovation clearly and fast.

Structure: what need → how we solve → what benefit → why better than rivals.

Used in pitches, grants, R&D and startup talks.
