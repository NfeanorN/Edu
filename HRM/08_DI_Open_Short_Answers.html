DI-1
Network externalities · Q15
(i) Product becomes more valuable as more people use it (messenger, marketplace). (ii) Platforms grow via network effects (winner-take-all). Value Network (Stabell & Fjeldstad) = value from linking users.
DI-2
MVP · Lean Startup
(i) MVP = version that gives max validated learning about customers with min effort (Eric Ries). (ii) Build–Measure–Learn loop. Full product first = risk: big spend before demand is proven.
DI-3
Rogers · diffusion & free vs $20
(i) Diffusion = innovation spreads through communication in a social system (Rogers 1983). Attributes: relative advantage, compatibility, complexity, trialability, observability. Adopters: innovators → early majority → laggards.
(ii) Exam example: $20-only blocks trial → slow diffusion; free tier enables try + word-of-mouth → faster spread.
DI-4
TAM · PU & PEOU
TAM (Davis): Perceived Usefulness = belief system improves job performance. Perceived Ease of Use = belief system is free of effort. Both drive attitude and intention to use.
DI-5
Onlife · Floridi
Onlife = online and offline are merged; asking «online or offline?» no longer makes sense. Digital layer is always part of everyday life (Floridi).
DI-6
Chain · Shop · Network
Value Chain = transform inputs into products (factory). Value Shop = solve unique client problems (hospital, consulting). Value Network = link interdependent users (platform, telecom).
DI-7
Platform vs pipeline
Pipeline firm makes product and sells step by step. Platform connects two sides (buyers/sellers) and gains value from network effects as users grow.
DI-8
Double Diamond
Double Diamond: Discover (diverge) → Define (converge) → Develop (diverge) → Deliver (converge). Diverge = open options; converge = narrow choice.
DI-9
Agile vs Waterfall
Waterfall = sequential phases (requirements → design → build → test). Agile = iterative sprints, frequent delivery, continuous feedback.
DI-10
Blockchain
Blockchain = distributed ledger replicated on many nodes; blocks linked by cryptography. Proof of Work secures chain. Trust without central authority. ≠ Bitcoin alone.
DI-11
Encryption
Encryption scrambles content so it cannot be read without the decryption key. Protects confidentiality. ≠ VPN (hides connection, not content).
DI-12
BPM
BPM = ongoing design, execution, monitoring and improvement of business processes. Needs process-oriented culture, not only software tools.
DI-13
LLM hallucination
Hallucination = LLM outputs fluent but false/fabricated content (fake facts, citations). Model predicts words, not verified truth — human verification needed.
DI-14
Industry 5.0
Industry 5.0 (Leng et al.) = human-centred collaboration with intelligent systems; well-being and sustainability, not automation alone.
DI-15
Dooley · CAO
Complex adaptive organisation suits turbulent environments with diverse actors — not stable/predictable ones. MCQ trap: «stable» or «low diversity» = wrong; often answer E (none).
