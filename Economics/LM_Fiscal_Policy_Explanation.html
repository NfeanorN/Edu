LM Curve & Fiscal Policy

Money market equilibrium / Равновесие на денежном рынке

Кривая LM — все (Y, i), при которых M^s = M^d: M = $Y · L(i).

The LM curve shows all (Y, i) where the money market clears: M^s = M^d = $Y · L(i).

  • Y ↑ → $Y ↑ → M^d ↑ → при фикс. M^s: i ↑
  • LM восходящая: больше Y требует выше iUpward-sloping LM

Higher output → higher nominal income → higher money demand → higher i for given M^s.

G↑ → IS сдвигается вправо → A→B на LM: Y↑ и i↑Expansionary fiscal: IS shifts right; new equilibrium B has higher Y and i.

Fiscal Policy on IS-LM / Фискальная политика

Expansionary fiscal (G ↑ or T ↓)

  • IS shifts right
  • Y ↑ and i ↑ (crowding out частично: i↑ → I↓)

Expansionary fiscal: IS shifts right → output and interest rate both rise. Higher i may reduce private investment (crowding out).

Contractionary fiscal (G ↓ or T ↑)

  • IS shifts left
  • Y ↓ and i ↓

Contractionary fiscal: IS left → Y and i both fall.

G ↑ / T ↓→ right↑↑
G ↓ / T ↑→ left↓↓

More government spending raises Y but also i. Less spending lowers both.

Crowding out: G↑ → Y↑ → M^d↑ → i↑ → I↓ частично компенсирует рост Y.

Crowding out: fiscal expansion raises i, which dampens private investment.

LM: M^s = M^d · upward slope · fiscal → IS shift · G↑: Y↑ i↑ (crowding out)

LM upward. Fiscal policy shifts IS only. Expansion raises Y and i.

LM — денежный рынок. Фискальная политика сдвигает IS: G↑ → Y↑ и i↑.

LM = money market equilibrium. Fiscal policy shifts IS: more G raises Y and i.
