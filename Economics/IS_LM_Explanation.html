IS-LM Model — Модель IS-LM

What is IS-LM? / Что такое IS-LM?

English: Model of goods market (IS) and money market (LM) together. Determines equilibrium Y and interest rate i simultaneously.

Русский: Модель рынка товаров (IS) и денежного рынка (LM). Определяет равновесные Y и i.

IS Curve (Goods Market) / Кривая IS (Рынок товаров)

IS = combinations of (Y, i) where goods market is in equilibrium (Z = Y).

Slope negative: Higher i → less investment → less demand → lower Y.

Y = C(Y) + I(i) + G — I depends negatively on i

Кривая IS — нисходящая: рост i → падение I → падение Y.

Shifts: G↑ or T↓ → IS shifts right. G↓ or T↑ → IS shifts left.

LM Curve (Money Market) / Кривая LM (Денежный рынок)

LM = combinations of (Y, i) where money market is in equilibrium (M/P = L(Y,i)).

Slope positive: Higher Y → more money demand → higher i (for given M).

M/P = L(Y, i) — L rises with Y, falls with i

Кривая LM — восходящая: рост Y → рост спроса на деньги → рост i.

Shifts: M↑ → LM shifts right. M↓ → LM shifts left.

Equilibrium / Равновесие

Intersection of IS and LM = simultaneous equilibrium in both markets.

E — равновесие (Y*, i*). Оба рынка сбалансированы одновременно.

Numerical Example — Find Y* and i* / Числовой пример

Exam Prep #26. Typical problem: you are given C, I, G (goods market) and M, L (money market). Solve two equations, two unknowns (Y and i).

Given data

C = 50 + 0.75Y  ·  I = 200 − 20i  ·  G = 50

M = 200 (money supply)  ·  L = 0.25Y − 5i (money demand)

i = interest rate (e.g. %). Closed economy, no taxes in this simplified setup.

Step 1 — Derive the IS curve (goods market: Y = Z)

Y = C + I + G
Y = 50 + 0.75Y + 200 − 20i + 50
Y − 0.75Y = 300 − 20i
0.25Y = 300 − 20i

IS:  Y = 1 200 − 80i   (or:  0.25Y + 20i = 300)

Step 2 — Write the LM curve (money market: M = L)

200 = 0.25Y − 5i

LM:  0.25Y − 5i = 200   (or:  Y = 800 + 20i)

Step 3 — Solve simultaneously

Substitute IS into LM:
200 = 0.25(1 200 − 80i) − 5i
200 = 300 − 20i − 5i
200 = 300 − 25i
25i = 100

i* = 4

Y* = 1 200 − 80(4) = 1 200 − 320

Y* = 880

Check LM: 0.25(880) − 5(4) = 220 − 20 = 200 ✓

Shortcut if IS is already given as one line

Sometimes the exam gives Y = 500 + 0.5Y − 10i directly (already simplified). Then:

0.5Y = 500 − 10i  →  Y = 1 000 − 20i   (same IS, rescaled)

With LM: 200 = 0.25Y − 5i  →  i* = 5, Y* = 900

Always rearrange to Y = … or …Y + …i = constant before substituting.

What if central bank sells bonds? (M↓)

CB sells bonds → banks pay CB → M falls → LM shifts left → at old Y, money demand > supply → i↑ → I↓ → move up along IS → Y↓.

Example: M drops from 200 to 150 (same IS):

150 = 0.25(1 200 − 80i) − 5i
150 = 300 − 25i
25i = 150  →  i* = 6  (↑ from 4)
Y* = 1 200 − 80(6) = 720  (↓ from 880)
