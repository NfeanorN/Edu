Determinants & Price Controls

Frank Ch. 2, Fig. 2.9 · movement vs shift · ceiling & floor

Movement along curve — изменилась собственная цена P. Shift — изменился другой фактор → вся кривая D или S сдвигается.

Movement along the curve = own price changed. Shift = another factor changed → entire D or S curve moves.

Price ceiling / Потолок цены (maximum price)

Price ceiling — максимальная legal цена ниже P* → shortage (дефицит): Q_D > Q_S.

A price ceiling is a legal maximum price set below equilibrium → shortage: quantity demanded exceeds quantity supplied.

Ceiling ниже P* → shortage (Q_D > Q_S)Binding ceiling below equilibrium creates shortage.

Price floor / Пол цены (minimum price)

Price floor — минимальная legal цена выше P* → surplus (избыток): Q_S > Q_D.

A price floor is a legal minimum price set above equilibrium → surplus: quantity supplied exceeds quantity demanded.

Floor выше P* → surplus (Q_S > Q_D)Binding floor above equilibrium creates surplus.

Own P change = movement · Other factor = shift · Ceiling below P* = shortage · Floor above P* = surplus

Own price → movement along curve. Other factors → shift. Ceiling below P* → shortage. Floor above P* → surplus.

Сдвиг D или S меняет P* и Q*. Потолок цены (ниже равновесия) → дефицит. Пол цены (выше равновесия) → избыток.

Shifts change equilibrium. Price ceiling → shortage. Price floor → surplus.
