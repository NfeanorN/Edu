MRS — Marginal Rate of Substitution

Frank Ch. 3 · MRS = |slope of IC| · optimum: MRS = P_S/P_F

MRS (marginal rate of substitution) — скорость, с которой потребитель готов обменять один товар на другой, оставаясь на одной indifference curve (same utility).

MRS is the rate at which the consumer is willing to trade one good for another along an indifference curve — staying equally satisfied.

MRS = |slope of IC| = |ΔF / ΔS|   (on IC, holding U constant)

MRS equals the absolute value of the slope of the indifference curve.

Example / Пример из конспекта

I have 5 apples and 2 bananas

Willing to give up 2 apples to get 1 banana

MRS = 2 (apples per banana — сколько apples отдаёшь за 1 banana)

Example: willing to trade 2 apples for 1 banana → MRS = 2.

Later: more shelter, less food — Food=6, Shelter=4

Now willing to give up only 1 food for 1 shelter

MRS = 1 (changes along the IC — diminishing MRS)

As you move along the IC, MRS usually falls (diminishing MRS) — convex curve.

MRS on indifference curve / MRS на IC

A → B вдоль IC: MRS падает (diminishing MRS) · slope IC = willingness to tradeMoving down the IC, MRS falls — convex to origin.

Optimum: MRS = price ratio / Оптимум

At best bundle F:   MRS = P_S / P_F = 1/2   (Frank: P_S=$5, P_F=$10)
Willingness to trade = market trade-off · same steepness: IC tangent to budget line

At optimum, MRS equals the price ratio. Consumer's willingness to trade equals the market rate.

Budget line shifts & M / MRS / Сдвиги budget

Income M ↑ or ↓ — parallel shift

  • Income ↑ (M↑) → budget line shifts outward (parallel)
  • Income ↓ (M↓) → budget line shifts inward
  • Slope unchanged (same prices) · can reach higher IC

Higher income → parallel outward shift. Lower income → inward. Slope stays −P_S/P_F.

M↑ → budget outward (parallel) · M↓ → inward · intercepts (0,10) and (20,0) scaleIncome changes shift budget parallel; slope unchanged.

Price change — rotation (slope changes)

  • Relative prices change → budget line rotates (slope changes)
  • Example: F intercept 20→21, S intercept 10→9 — new trade-off

Price change → rotation around one intercept · slope (MRS at market) changesDifferent slopes = different price ratios.

MRS = |slope IC| = willingness to trade · diminishes along IC · optimum: MRS = P_S/P_F · M↑ → parallel shift · price change → rotation

Key: MRS on IC, equals price ratio at optimum. Income shifts budget parallel; price changes rotate it.
