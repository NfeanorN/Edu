Topic 20: Long-Run Cost Function & Returns to Scale

Source: Frank, Ch. 9 — Figure 9.13

LRAC = envelope of many short-run ATC curves — each SR curve is for one plant size.

Frank (Ch. 9, p. 286): shape of LRAC predicts industry structure — falling LRAC (increasing RTS) → one big firm tends toward monopoly; rising LRAC → many small firms.

SR ATC envelope

Each SR ATC is for one plant size. LRAC touches the lowest point on each SR curve — firm picks optimal scale for each output level.

🇷🇺 По-русски

LRAC — огибающая краткосрочных ATC. Форма LRAC предсказывает структуру отрасли: падающая LRAC → монополия/крупные фирмы; растущая → много мелких (Frank, гл. 9).
