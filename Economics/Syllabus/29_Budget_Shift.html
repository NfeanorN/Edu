Topic 29: Budget Constraint — Shift When Price Falls

Source: Frank, Ch. 3

Budget line: PS·S + PF·F = M. Intercepts: M/PS on shelter axis, M/PF on food axis.

If PF falls (food cheaper), the food intercept M/PF rises — the budget line rotates outward on the food axis; shelter intercept unchanged. Consumer can reach higher indifference curves (Ch. 3, price and income changes).

Also: income increase

If M rises (income ↑) with unchanged prices → parallel outward shift of entire budget line (both intercepts rise proportionally).

Если P_F падает — бюджетная линия поворачивается наружу по оси food (перехват M/P_F растёт). Если растёт доход M — параллельный сдвиг всей линии наружу. Shelter/food, Frank гл. 3.
