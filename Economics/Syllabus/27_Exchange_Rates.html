Topic 27: Real vs Nominal Exchange Rate

Source: Macroeconomics course

Open-economy macro — not in Frank.

Nominal exchange rate: price of foreign currency in domestic currency.

Real exchange rate: adjusts nominal rate for price levels at home and abroad — measures relative purchasing power of goods.

Real exchange rate ≈ (e × Pforeign) / Pdomestic

Higher real rate → foreign goods cheaper relative to home goods.

🇷🇺 По-русски

Номинальный курс — цена иностранной валюты. Реальный курс — с поправкой на уровни цен в странах; показывает относительную покупательную способность товаров.
