Topic 17: Investment vs Saving

Source: Macroeconomics course

Macro identity — not in Frank micro chapters.
Saving (S)Investment (I)
WhoHouseholds (and government surplus)Firms buying capital
MeaningIncome not consumed nowSpending on machines, buildings, inventory
Closed economy equilibrium: S = I · Y = C + I + G + NX

Loanable funds intuition

Households save → banks lend → firms invest. If S > I, r falls until equilibrium. If I > S, r rises.

Full chapters: Goods Market Multiplier Explanation

🇷🇺 По-русски

Сбережения S — доход минус потребление. Инвестиции I — покупка капитала фирмами. В закрытой экономике в равновесии S = I. S финансирует I через финансовую систему.
