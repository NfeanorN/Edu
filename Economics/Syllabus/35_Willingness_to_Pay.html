Topic 35: Willingness to Pay (WTP)

Source: Frank, Ch. 2 & 5 — Figure 5.4

WTP = maximum a buyer would pay for a unit. The demand curve shows marginal WTP at each quantity.

Ch. 5 (Figure 5.4): consumer surplus = area between demand curve and price paid — difference between WTP and actual price for all units bought. Lobster market: buyers who would pay up to $20 but pay $8 gain surplus.

Total vs marginal WTP

Demand height at each Q = marginal WTP of that unit. CS = sum of (WTP − P) for all units bought = triangle under D above P.

WTP (готовность платить) — максимум за единицу. Кривая спроса = предельная WTP. Излишек потребителя (рис. 5.4) — площадь между D и ценой P до Q*. Покупатель лобстера за $8 с WTP $20 получает surplus $12.
