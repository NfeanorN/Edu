Topic 22: When Is Marginal Utility Maximized?

Source: Frank, Ch. 3

MU for one good is highest at very low consumption, then falls (diminishing MU). But the consumer does not choose max MU — they choose max utility subject to the budget.

Optimum: MUx/Px = MUy/Py — last dollar spent on each good gives equal extra utility. MU alone can be high for bread and low for water, but you still buy both because prices differ.

Exam answer in one sentence

MU is maximized for a single good at zero consumption, but the consumer optimum equalizes MU/P across goods at the budget tangency — not max MU of one good.

Full chapters: Rational Choice Explanation EN

🇷🇺 По-русски

MU одного товара высока только при малом потреблении, но оптимум потребителя — это максимум общей полезности при бюджете: MUₓ/Pₓ = MUᵧ/Pᵧ, а не «максимизировать MU» одного товара.
