Topic 10: How Does the Interest Rate Affect GDP?

Source: Macroeconomics course

Macro topic — not in Frank textbook.

Higher r: borrowing is expensive → C ↓ (mortgages, cars) and I ↓ (business investment) → aggregate demand ↓ → GDP (Y) ↓ → less inflation pressure.

Lower r: the opposite chain → GDP ↑.

Chain: r → C and I → AD → Y (GDP). On IS-LM: higher r moves equilibrium left (lower Y).

Components of AD

Y = C + I + G + NX. Higher r mainly hits C (mortgages) and I (business loans). Stronger currency can reduce NX.

🇷🇺 По-русски

Рост процентной ставки → дороже заимствования → потребление C и инвестиции I падают → совокупный спрос AD ↓ → ВВП Y ↓. Снижение r — обратная цепочка. На графике IS-LM: r↑ → точка равновесия левее (меньше Y).
