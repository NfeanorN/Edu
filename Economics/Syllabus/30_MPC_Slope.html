Topic 30: MPC & Slope of Consumption Function

Source: Macroeconomics course

Macro consumption function — not Frank.
C = c₀ + c₁·Y · c₁ = MPC = slope of C(Y) line

c₀ = autonomous consumption (spending when Y = 0). c₁ = fraction of each extra euro of income spent.

Autonomous consumption c₀

Even at Y = 0, households spend c₀ (essentials, borrowing, savings drawdown) — see Exam Q&A #3.

C = c₀ + c₁Y. c₀ — автономное потребление (траты при Y=0). c₁ = MPC — наклон прямой C(Y). MPS = 1 − MPC.
