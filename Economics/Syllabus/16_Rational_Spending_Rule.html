Topic 16: Rational Spending Rule

Source: Frank, Ch. 3 — Figure 3.15

MUS/PS = MUF/PF equivalently MRS = PS/PF
Shelter & food, M = $100/wk, PS = $5/sq yd, PF = $10/lb. At a point where IC slope (MRS) = 1/4 but budget slope = 1/2, the consumer should buy more food and less shelter until rates match (Figure 3.15).

Numeric check (Ch. 3)

If MUF/PF = 8/10 = 0.8 and MUS/PS = 3/5 = 0.6 → buy more food (higher MU per dollar) until equal.

🇷🇺 По-русски

Рациональное правило расходов: MU_S/P_S = MU_F/P_F, или MRS = P_S/P_F. Пример Frank: M=$100/нед, P_S=$5, P_F=$10. Если MRS < P_S/P_F — покупай больше food, меньше shelter до касания (рис. 3.15).
