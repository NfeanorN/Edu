Topic 15: Perfect Competition

Source: Frank, Ch. 10 — Figures 10.2, 10.4

Many small firms, identical product, free entry — each firm is a price taker.

Frank (Ch. 10): if one wheat farmer raises price by a penny, buyers switch — so P = MR = AR for the firm. Profit max: P = MC. Shutdown rule: produce only if P ≥ AVCmin (Figure 10.4). Long run: entry drives economic profit to zero (p. 321).

Conditions checklist

  • Many small firms
  • Homogeneous product
  • Free entry and exit
  • Perfect information
  • Price taker → horizontal demand for one firm

LR vs SR

SR: may earn π > 0 or π < 0. LR: π = 0 at min LRAC.

🇷🇺 По-русски

Совершенная конкуренция (Frank, гл. 10): много мелких фирм, одинаковый товар, свободный вход. Фирма — price taker: P = MR. Максимум прибыли: P = MC. Закрытие если P < AVCmin. Долгий период: экономическая прибыль = 0.
