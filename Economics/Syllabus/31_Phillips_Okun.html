Topic 31: Okun's Law & Phillips Curve

Source: Macroeconomics course

Macro — not in Frank.

Okun's Law: when GDP growth is below potential, unemployment rises (rough rule: 1% extra unemployment ↔ ~2% GDP gap).

Phillips curve (short run): lower unemployment ↔ higher inflation. Long run: vertical at natural rate — no trade-off.

Stagflation

1970s: SR Phillips seemed to break — high unemployment and high inflation → LR Phillips vertical (no long-run trade-off).

Закон Оукена: рост ниже потенциала → безработица растёт (~1% u ↔ 2% разрыв ВВП). Кривая Филлипса (КП): в КП u↓ → π↑. В долгом периоде КП вертикальна при естественном уровне u — компромисса нет.
