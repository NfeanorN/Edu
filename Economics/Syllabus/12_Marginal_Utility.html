Topic 12: Marginal Utility (MU)

Source: Frank, Ch. 1 & 3

Marginal utility (MU) = extra satisfaction from one more unit of a good.

Ch. 1 (Figure 1.1): marginal benefit of talking on the phone falls as you talk longer — same idea as diminishing MU for goods. Ch. 3: rational rule uses MU per dollar, not raw MU.
Rational spending: MUx/Px = MUy/Py
You do NOT maximize MU alone — you equalize MU/P across goods (Exam topic 22).

Water vs diamonds paradox

Total utility of water is high, but marginal utility of the next glass is low when you are not thirsty — rational choice uses MU/P, not MU alone.

🇷🇺 По-русски

Предельная полезность MU — прирост удовлетворения от ещё одной единицы. Обычно убывает. Рациональное правило: MUₓ/Pₓ = MUᵧ/Pᵧ — сравниваем полезность на последний потраченный евро, а не «максимизируем MU» одного товара.
