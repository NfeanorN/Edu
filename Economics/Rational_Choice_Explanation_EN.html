Rational Choice

Source: Robert H. Frank, Microeconomics and Behavior, 7th ed. — Chapter 1 (cost-benefit principle, Figure 1.1) and Chapter 3 «Rational Consumer Choice» (budget constraint, indifference curves, best affordable bundle). All examples and numbers are from the textbook.

Frank describes rational choice in two linked frameworks: (1) the general rule «act while MB ≥ MC» (Ch. 1); (2) the consumer model with shelter and food at income M = $100/wk (Ch. 3).

Part I — Cost-Benefit Principle (Ch. 1)

1. Cost-Benefit Principle Ch. 1, Figure 1.1

The optimal amount of any continuously variable activity is where marginal benefit MB = marginal cost MC.

Frank (Figure 1.1): MB of conversation falls with duration; MC is flat at 4¢/min. Optimum = 400 minutes/month where MB crosses MC.

Figure 1.1 (Frank): optimum at MB = MC. For a consumer, MB of the last unit = MU and MC = P.

Part II — Rational Consumer Choice (Ch. 3)

2. Rational Consumer Ch. 3

A rational consumer has well-defined preferences, knows prices and income, and picks the bundle that maximizes utility among affordable options.

Indifference curve properties (Ch. 3)

3. Utility Function Ch. 3, Appendix

Frank (Appendix): utility for food F (lb/wk) and shelter S (sq yd/wk). Example: U(F, S) = F · S (utils per week).

F = 4 lb/wk, S = 3 sq yd/wk → U = 12 utils/wk. F = 8, S = 6 → U = 48 utils/wk. Bundles (4,3) and (3,4) both yield 12 utils — same IC.

For U = FS: MUF = S, MUS = F

4. Budget Constraint Ch. 3

M = $100/wk, PS = $5/sq yd, PF = $10/lb:

5S + 10F = 100 · slope = −PS/PF = −1/2

Intercept shelter: K = (20, 0) · intercept food: L = (0, 10)

Textbook bundles: A = (5 sq yd, 7 lb); B = (3 sq yd, 8 lb). On the line: E = (12, 4). Below the line: D = (5, 4) (income not fully spent).

5. Consumer Equilibrium Ch. 3, Figure 3.15

MRS = PS / PF = 1/2

At point F (best affordable bundle): slope of IC = slope of budget line.

Equivalent (Appendix): MUF/PF = MUS/PS

At point E on the budget line, IC slope might be 1/4 < 1/2 — the consumer should move toward F, trading shelter for food until MRS = 1/2.
Ch. 1: MB = MC (Figure 1.1). Ch. 3: MRS = PS/PF or MUF/PF = MUS/PS with M = $100, PS = $5, PF = $10.
