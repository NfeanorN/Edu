Money Demand, Money Supply & Equilibrium Interest Rate

На денежном рынке M^s = M^d определяет равновесную ставку i*.

On the money market, M^s = M^d determines the equilibrium interest rate i*.

M = $Y · L(i)
$Y — nominal income (P × Y) · L(i) — money demand (↓ when i ↑)

M = nominal income × money demand function L(i). L decreases when interest rate rises.

M^s — вертикальная (задаёт ЦБ). M^d — ↓ при i↑. A = равновесие.M^s vertical (CB). M^d downward-sloping. A = equilibrium i*.

  • i ↑ → M^d ↓ (дороже держать cash, лучше bonds)
  • i ↓ → M^d ↑
  • M^s = M^d → equilibrium i

Higher i → less money demand. Lower i → more money demand. M^s = M^d at equilibrium.

Shifts / Сдвиги

M^s ↑ (CB buys bonds)

  • CB buys bonds → M^s ↑ → i ↓

Central bank buys bonds → money supply increases → people have more money → interest rate decreases.

$Y ↑ (nominal income)

  • $Y ↑ → M^d ↑ (больше транзакций) → M^s same → i ↑

Nominal income up → people hold more money → money demand shifts right → i rises.

Banks & Central bank money / Банки

Банки: deposits, reserves (наличные + счёт в ЦБ), loans.

Banks receive deposits, keep reserves, and make loans. Bonds pay interest.

H^d = θ · M^d = θ · $Y · L(i)   |   θ = reserve ratio
H^d — demand for central bank money · θ (theta) — % deposits kept as reserves

Demand for reserves = θ × money demand. More deposits → more reserves needed.

M^s vertical · M^d ↓ with i · M = $Y·L(i) · CB buy bonds → M↑ i↓ · $Y↑ → i↑

Money supply fixed by CB. Demand slopes down. Nominal income shifts demand.

Денежный рынок: ЦБ задаёт M^s, спрос L(i) зависит от i и $Y. Равновесие → i*.

Money market: CB sets M^s, demand depends on i and nominal income. Equilibrium gives i*.
