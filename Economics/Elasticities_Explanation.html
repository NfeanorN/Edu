Elasticities of Demand

Source: Frank, Ch. 4 — «Demand and Elasticity». Examples tie to the lobster market (Ch. 2) and monopoly pricing (Ch. 11: MR = P(1 − 1/|ε|)).

1. Price elasticity of demand (PED)

PED = (% ΔQd) / (% ΔP) — always negative for normal goods; we often use |PED|.
|PED|NameMeaning
> 1ElasticQuantity reacts more than price (flatter D curve)
= 1Unit elasticSame % change in Q and P
< 1InelasticQuantity reacts less than price (steeper D)
Ch. 2 lobsters: if demand is elastic, a price cut raises total revenue (more buyers). Ch. 11: a monopolist never chooses the inelastic part of D because MR < 0 there — cutting Q would raise revenue and save cost.

2. Income elasticity (YED)

YED = (% ΔQd) / (% ΔIncome)
YEDGood type
> 0Normal good — buy more when income rises
< 0Inferior good — buy less when income rises
> 1Luxury
Ch. 2 (Figure 2.9): higher income shifts lobster demand right — normal good. Exam macro also uses YED; see Exam Q&A.

3. Cross-price elasticity (XED)

XED = (% ΔQA) / (% ΔPB)
Ch. 2: crabs and lobsters are substitutes (XED > 0) — if crab price rises, lobster demand shifts right. Butter with lobsters is a complement (XED < 0).

4. Elasticity and total revenue

Elastic: price ↓ → total revenue ↑. Inelastic: price ↓ → total revenue ↓. This is why monopolists care about |ε| (Ch. 11).

🇷🇺 По-русски

Эластичность (Frank, гл. 4): PED = %ΔQ/%ΔP. |ε|>1 эластичный, <1 неэластичный. YED — нормальный/нижний товар. XED — заменители (+) и дополняющие (−). Связь с TR и монополией (MR и ε). Лобстеры и крабы — XED.
