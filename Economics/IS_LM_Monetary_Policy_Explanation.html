IS-LM Equilibrium & Monetary Policy

Точка A — пересечение IS и LM: одновременное равновесие на рынке товаров и денежном рынке.

Point A = intersection of IS and LM: goods market and money market both in equilibrium.

CB buys bonds → M^s↑ → LM сдвигается вниз-вправо → A→B: Y↑ i↓Expansionary monetary: LM shifts down/right; new equilibrium B has higher Y, lower i.

Monetary Policy / Денежно-кредитная политика

Expansionary (CB buys bonds, M^s ↑)

  • CB buys bonds → M^s ↑ → LM shifts down (вниз-вправо)
  • Y ↑ and i ↓

Central bank buys bonds → money supply up → LM shifts right → output rises, interest rate falls.

Contractionary (CB sells bonds, M^s ↓)

  • CB sells bonds → M^s ↓ → LM shifts left
  • Y ↓ and i ↑

Central bank sells bonds → M down → LM left → Y falls, i rises.
