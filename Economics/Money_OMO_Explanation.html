Open Market Operations (OMO) / Операции на открытом рынке

OMO — покупка и продажа облигаций центральным банком.

Open market operations = buying and selling bonds by the central bank.

  • CB buys bonds → M ↑ → i ↓
  • CB sells bonds → M ↓ → i ↑

Central bank buys bonds → money supply up → interest rate down. Sells bonds → M down → i up.

PolicyPolicyДействие CBCB actionЭффектEffect
Expansionary OMOПокупает облигацииBuys bondsM ↑, i ↓Stimulates economy
Contractionary OMOПродаёт облигацииSells bondsM ↓, i ↑Slows economy

Expansionary: buy bonds, increase M. Contractionary: sell bonds, decrease M.

Bonds / Облигации

Bond — финансовый актив, который платит процент.

A bond is a financial asset that pays interest.

Покупаешь bond за $1000

Через год: получаешь $1000 обратно + $50 interest

Return = $50 / $1000 = 5%

Buy a bond for $1000. One year later get $1000 back plus $50 interest.

Flow: income, saving · Stock: wealth · OMO: buy → M↑ i↓ · sell → M↓ i↑

Key: flows vs stocks. OMO moves money supply and interest rate in opposite directions.

ЦБ управляет M через OMO. Покупка облигаций = экспансия, продажа = сдерживание.

The central bank controls M through OMO. Bond purchases expand; sales contract.
