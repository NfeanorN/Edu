Monetary-Fiscal Policy Mix

Combine policies to hit specific Y and i targets / Комбинация для целевых Y и i

Можно комбинировать политики: например T ↑ (сдерживание) + M^s ↑ (стимул) → Y unchanged, но i ↓.

Policy mix: e.g. raise taxes (contractionary fiscal) plus increase money supply (expansionary monetary) → keep Y the same but lower i.

T↑ → IS влево · M↑ → LM вниз-вправо · C: Y = Y_A, i нижеTax hike shifts IS left; money expansion shifts LM down/right — same Y, lower i at C.

Example from notebook / Пример из конспекта

  • T ↑ (налоги) → IS shifts left → Y ↓, i ↓
  • M^s ↑ (CB buys bonds) → LM shifts right → Y ↑, i ↓
  • Together: effects on Y offset → Y unchanged, net effect: i ↓

Higher taxes shift IS left (lower Y, lower i). More money shifts LM right (higher Y, lower i). Combined: Y stays, i falls.

Why use a mix? / Зачем микс?

  • Снизить deficit / сдержать inflation expectations (T↑)
  • Поддержать инвестиции через низкий i (M↑)
  • Избежать crowding out при чисто fiscal expansion

Mix can tighten fiscal side while keeping output stable and cutting interest rates to support investment.
